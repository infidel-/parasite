## Profane Ordeals

Spawn on their own: 20% per turn once the cult has 3+ members and 2+ free members, at most 1 active (2 once the cult has 6+ members), none while a combat one is active, and an 11–14 turn cooldown after one ends. Each assigns 2 random members (level 1 preferred), runs an 8–12 turn timer, carries one random penalty while it runs (lower power of its type, lower income, or communal ordeals blocked), and on failure adds a random bad effect for 5 turns (10 at 5% chance).

Power tier: 25 power and 100000 money if the cult has 2+ level 3 members, else 10 and 50000 if it has 2+ level 2 members, else 5 and 20000. Both values vary ±10%; money is then rounded down to 1000.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Defend Cor Nefandum | 0 (a rival cult's attack pressure reaches 30) | none; completes through the base defense mission |
| Defend Cor Nefandum (The Group) | 0 (a Group team attacks the base) | none; completes through the team base defense mission |
